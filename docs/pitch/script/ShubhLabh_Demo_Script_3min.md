## 1. Creative concept and tone

**Concept (one line):** *Chhapne se pehle sudhaarein — fix it before you print it.* Open on one honest number arriving on WhatsApp in under a minute; then reveal the engine behind it turning a single click into a festival campaign where every rupee is verified before a person approves it.

**Tone:** a premium product-launch keynote, not an advertisement. Calm, confident, specific. Numbers are read the way a CFO reads them. English for an Indian corporate audience; Hinglish only where the product itself speaks it (the WhatsApp reply, the typed note, the tagline). No hype words, no exclamation marks in the VO.

**Why a judge leans in at 0:17:** after the 13-s live-link card, the first thing on screen is not a logo but a shopkeeper about to print a wrong number — and an AI correcting it in his own language, with the maths. The rest of the film shows the same engine at the scale of a grocery chain.

**Visual grammar:** the live app recorded at 1600 × 1000, 100 % zoom. Push-ins and highlights are done in the edit on the screen recording. Supers: Poppins SemiBold, white on a 70 % navy (#0B1A5C) lower-third bar, at most two lines; key numbers in saffron (#F28A1A). ★ marks a super that carries a key number — give those at least 3 s. One small-print line sits under every rupee figure: *Rupee figures are estimated on ShubhLabh's reference store data.*

**Sound:** one original bed, no vocals: warm cinematic pad, soft tabla pulse, restrained low brass under the big numbers. The bed drops out for half a second at the two reveals (the honest "4%" and the kit badge) so the chime lands alone. SFX are the product's own vocabulary: WhatsApp tones, UI clicks, one tick per pipeline step, a low thud for anything blocked.
