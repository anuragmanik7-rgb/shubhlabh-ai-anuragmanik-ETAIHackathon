# Three-minute demonstration

Use a new preserved workspace and a prepared current synthetic event. Keep labels visible. Record the actual application; do not substitute generated success screens.

**0:00–0:20 — The problem.** A grocery category manager needs profitable promotions across Bengaluru, Mumbai and Delhi while stock expires and competitor prices change. Show the synthetic catalog, contribution floor and shared budget. A blanket discount ignores portfolio losses.

**0:20–0:50 — Evidence.** Open the original policy PDF and competitor image. With live Gemini configured, extract and confirm their fields against the source. Show the date/pack match and the human confirmation boundary. If live inference is unavailable, explicitly demonstrate manual review and state that this recording is offline.

**0:50–1:25 — Plan.** Generate the seven-day brief. Show actual persisted tool progress, excluded low-margin products, audience, duration, city and candidate types. Explain that CP-SAT selects from a bounded offer set and the separate simulator can reject an attractive-looking plan.

**1:25–1:55 — Portfolio outcomes.** Compare optimized, flat-discount and no-promotion outcomes. Show full-portfolio contribution and P10/downside. Open a decision record with substitute loss, costs and source references. State that uplift is simulated, not a retailer result.

**1:55–2:30 — Controlled action and repair.** Approve the displayed revision, activate in the sandbox and redeem one valid offer. Inject an inventory drop or competitor event. Show the previous and new revision, changed offers and stale approval rejection. Enable automatic activation only inside the declared sandbox envelope.

**2:30–3:00 — Proof and limits.** Show test evidence, nine feature mappings and checkpoint recovery. Integrations display Flipkart as not connected. Explain the Replit deployment approach and what a retailer pilot would validate. Do not claim a live external integration, guaranteed uplift or D3 reliability beyond measured evidence.
